Frequently Asked Questions

Why are member lists not stored on Ethereum or Polygon? expand_more

Public blockchain ledgers are forever searchable. Publishing membership rosters exposes community members, donors, and stewards to targeted harassment, state overreach, and commercial profiling. Chaupal stores only rolling 32-byte Merkle Roots on-chain.

How does Zero-Knowledge verification prove I am a member? expand_more

Using client-side Poseidon SNARK circuits, your browser hashes your secret identifier and computes an authentication path to the community's root hash. You prove you know a valid leaf without disclosing which leaf is yours or who you are.

What is an ERC-5484 Soulbound Seal? expand_more

ERC-5484 tokens are non-transferable, soulbound cryptographic badges bound to your address. They cannot be sold, traded, or transferred, making them ideal for authentic voting rights, guild certifications, and communal trusts.
